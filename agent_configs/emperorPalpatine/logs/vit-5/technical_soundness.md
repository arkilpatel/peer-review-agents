With all due respect for the authors' empirical observations, I am compelled to voice my concerns regarding the technical soundness of the arguments presented in this work. A rigorous scientific manuscript must possess an unbroken chain of logic linking evidence to its claims, and I humbly fear several gaps remain.

My primary concern centers on the diagnosis of the "over-gating" phenomenon described in Section 3.3. The authors claim that combining LayerScale and SwiGLU MLP leads to "excessively sparse activations" and attribute a drop in performance to this sparsity. However, this is a classic post hoc ergo propter hoc fallacy. The authors present a table showing a modest degradation in accuracy, but they provide absolutely no empirical measurement of activation sparsity, nor any theoretical proof that gating-induced sparsity is the sole culprit. Concluding that the mechanism is "over-gating" without visualizing or quantifying the intermediate representation distributions is an unjustified leap in logic. 

Furthermore, the theoretical justification for applying a high-frequency RoPE base to register tokens is quite delicate. The authors state that unrotated register tokens "naturally exhibit lower cosine similarity with patches that undergo smaller rotational angles." Yet, register tokens are by definition spatially agnostic global tokens. Attempting to assign them a relative position via a high-frequency heuristic is mathematically incongruent with their purpose. While it may empirically stabilize attention, the stated reasoning feels like a post-hoc rationalization for an ad-hoc fix, rather than a mathematically rigorous derivation.

Finally, I must respectfully point out that the claim regarding ViT-5's "significantly stronger resolution robustness" (Figure 3) is largely a well-documented artifact of using relative positional embeddings (RoPE) rather than absolute ones. Presenting this as a unique capability of the "ViT-5" architecture, rather than an inherent property of a previously published component (RoPE), slightly misattributes the technical mechanism driving the robustness. I would be immensely grateful if the authors could ground their claims with deeper analytical rigor.
