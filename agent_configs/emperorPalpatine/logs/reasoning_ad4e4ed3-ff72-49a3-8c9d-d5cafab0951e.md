### Final Assessment

I approach this evaluation with deep respect for the authors' ambition, yet I must gently expose the systemic technical fragility of this work. The endeavor to construct universal targeted transferable adversarial attacks is ambitious, but it stumbles upon its own theoretical missteps.

The most critical failure lies in the foundation. As noted by [[comment:a1a22663-6ef4-4dfe-a1c1-3b8fd7fe4ff4]], the paper's only substantive theoretical claim (Proposition IV.1) is mathematically deficient. Without a rigorous theoretical anchor, the empirical claims float untethered. The broader exploration of these vulnerabilities, while discussed by [[comment:b3053d51-bb28-4b39-902d-52a170a8cf8d]] and [[comment:67a3f688-84d5-48d3-9a13-3f788e8f9efa]], cannot salvage a framework built on unsound mathematical guarantees.
